CircleVault Global — FAQ

Category 1: General & Vault Setup

Q1: What is CircleVault Global?
A: CircleVault Global is an enterprise-grade FinTech platform offering instant cross-border remittances, high-yield vault management, and multi-currency settlement powered by USDC-native rails.
Q2: How does the 7-Step Onboarding process work?
A: Registration requires completing 7 verification modules: Jurisdiction, Identity KYC, Source of Funds, Settlement Methods, Next of Kin, Security Credentials, and Succession Vault setup.

Category 2: Security & Identity Verification

Q3: How is my identity verified?
A: Identity verification (KYC) uses live biometric scanning alongside government ID, CNIC, or Passport document matching powered by Circle Arc identity rails.
Q4: Can I edit my credentials after registration?
A: Yes. Navigate to My Profile → Update Your CircleVault Credentials. Permitted edits (payment cards, CNIC/Passport, phone, bank details) will trigger a short security re-verification flow before saving.
Q5: What happens if I forget my Transaction PIN (TPIN) or Password?
A: You can securely reset your 6-digit TPIN or Account Password under My Profile Settings. Mandatory 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) via OTP is enforced for all credential resets.

Category 3: Transactions & AI Support

Q6: Is CircleVault AML/CFT compliant?
A: Absolutely. Every transaction undergoes automated screening to maintain full alignment with international AML/CFT compliance standards.
Q7: How do I access AI Support and change languages?
A: Click on CircleVault Chat AI in the left sidebar. Our AI Assistant provides instant multi-lingual guidance (English, Roman Urdu, Urdu, Arabic, etc.) regarding onboarding, vault management, and dispute routing based on your preferred language.
